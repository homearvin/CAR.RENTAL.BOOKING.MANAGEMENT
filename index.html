Questions

Before you try the demo.

Is the demo a real business?

No. It's a sample rental business with made-up clients and bookings. Click anything you like. Nothing you do is saved and nothing is downloaded.

What is the Google notice at the top of the demo?

The system runs on Google, so Google shows a small notice saying the app was made by a Google Apps Script user (in Filipino: "Ang application na ito ay ginawa ng Google Apps Script user"). That's normal. Close it with the × and continue.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It opens in the web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari). A laptop gives the best view.

Is ₱5,000 a monthly fee?

No. You pay ₱5,000 once and have lifetime access. There is no monthly subscription.

Will it show my business name instead of "DriveNest"?

Yes. "DriveNest" is only the demo's sample business. Your copy shows your company name, tagline and logo on the screens and invoices.
